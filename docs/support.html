Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload my discharge instructions?
From the Home tab, tap "Upload instructions" and either take a photo or choose a PDF from your files. CuraPath will extract your medications, activity restrictions, warning signs, and other care instructions automatically.

What if the app misreads my instructions?
Always double-check extracted details against your original paperwork, especially medication doses and timing. If something looks wrong, you can re-upload a clearer photo, or contact your care team directly using the provider phone number saved in the app.

How do I change my daily check-in or medication reminder times?
Go to Settings → Notifications to adjust reminder times or turn reminders on/off.

How do I delete my account and data?
Go to Settings and tap "Delete my account & data." This permanently removes your account and all associated health data immediately.

Is my health data secure?
Yes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how your data is stored, processed, and protected.
